# Public Claim Boundaries

This repository is intended to make selected professional work inspectable without publishing private product assets, controlled company records, employer-confidential information, or unsupported claims.

## What I am comfortable claiming publicly

- I have a manufacturing background spanning defense and high-volume consumer production.
- My experience includes frontline leadership, Autonomous Maintenance, Total Productive Maintenance, root-cause work, equipment reliability, training, and cross-functional improvement.
- Since 2025 I have directed independent AI-assisted product development work across KaelUX-related projects.
- My responsibilities include requirements, technical architecture and ownership/authority boundaries within authorized scope, implementation direction, testing/evaluation design, failure investigation, and evidence-backed acceptance decisions.
- KaelUX Writing Engine work includes multi-provider AI behavior, structured context, persistence/continuity, companion evaluation, and controlled release-state separation.
- One major companion behavioral-certification campaign exercised 343 cases; case count is not presented as a quality or productivity score.
- Emet includes software-backed work for inspection, classification, proof planning, evidence reuse/invalidation, durable evidence records, and advisory review.
- Riff & Rondo includes Practice/Campaign progression, authenticated persistence, notation, learner UX/accessibility, browser input work, and automated testing.

## What I am not claiming

- I am not presenting myself as a conventional senior software engineer or machine-learning researcher.
- I am not claiming manual authorship of every line of code; implementation is heavily AI-assisted.
- I am not using raw token volume, commit count, file count, or lines of code as proof of productivity or engineering quality.
- I am not claiming that every implemented capability is currently Production-released.
- I am not claiming that a local test pass proves current hosted or Production behavior.
- I am not claiming that automated tests alone establish subjective AI behavior or visual quality.
- I am not claiming independent security-auditor, legal, or compliance credentials.
- I am not treating historical planning material as proof that a future product or feature has been built.
- I am not using this portfolio to claim company ownership or company-binding commercial/public-release authority for KaelUX LLC.

## Information intentionally excluded

The public version of this portfolio should not contain:

- API keys, credentials, secrets, tokens, or environment values;
- private user/customer data;
- private manuscripts or creative content;
- employer-confidential operational records;
- private database contents;
- exploitable security findings or unremediated vulnerability detail;
- internal Drive links or controlled-record URLs;
- raw private repository logs or complete internal evidence bundles;
- unpublished proprietary prompts where disclosure could expose product internals;
- personal phone numbers or unnecessary personal information.

## Current-state caution

Product state changes over time. A case study may accurately describe an implemented and validated capability while a later package remains unreleased or while fresh hosted certification is still pending.

For that reason, the portfolio prefers durable descriptions of demonstrated work over claims such as "currently live," "Production-ready," or "fully complete" unless those claims are intentionally reverified before publication.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Where evidence is incomplete, the appropriate public statement is narrower — not more confident.

I prefer language such as:

- "implemented and tested";
- "validated in a controlled environment";
- "Preview-verified";
- "Production release not claimed";
- "current hosted state not freshly verified";

when those distinctions are material.

## Future additions

Any future screenshots, diagrams, source samples, executable demonstrations, or performance metrics added to this portfolio should be reviewed against these boundaries before publication.
