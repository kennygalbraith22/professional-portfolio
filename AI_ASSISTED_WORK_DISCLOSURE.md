# AI-Assisted Work Disclosure

AI assistance is a central part of the projects described in this portfolio.

I use tools including ChatGPT and Codex extensively for implementation, analysis, test generation, debugging support, documentation, and iterative technical work. I do not present AI-generated implementation as if I manually authored every line of code.

## My role

My contribution includes:

- defining product and operational problems;
- establishing requirements, constraints, and acceptance criteria;
- mapping technical architecture, ownership/authority, and dependency boundaries within authorized scope;
- breaking large objectives into executable work;
- directing AI-assisted implementation;
- reviewing generated changes and technical evidence;
- designing deterministic tests, negative-path checks, fault-injection scenarios, and human evaluations;
- investigating failures and deciding whether they represent implementation defects, proof defects, environment differences, or unsupported assumptions;
- deciding whether existing evidence remains valid after a change;
- separating implementation from certification, deployed verification, and release decisions.

## What AI assistance does not remove

AI can produce large amounts of plausible code and documentation quickly. That does not establish that the result is correct, integrated, durable, safe, or suitable for release.

The work represented here therefore treats generation and acceptance as different stages. Depending on the claim, evidence may include automated tests, controlled failure cases, exact-version checks, deployment verification, database or persistence proof, and human behavioral review.

## Why I disclose this

I want the portfolio to show the work accurately.

The relevant professional skill is not pretending that AI was absent. It is being able to define what should be built, direct the tools producing it, identify where they are wrong, establish evidence appropriate to the claim, and make disciplined decisions about what is actually complete.

## Claim boundary

This portfolio should not be interpreted as a claim that I personally hand-coded the underlying applications without assistance, nor as a claim of expertise I cannot explain independently.

I am prepared to discuss the requirements, architecture choices, testing strategy, failure cases, release boundaries, and lessons described in these case studies in detail.
